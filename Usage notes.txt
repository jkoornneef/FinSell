Two independent signals tracked:

Uptrend line break — Fits a line along ascending swing lows over 200 bars; checks if price (especially weekly close) has broken below
200-day MA turning down — Slope of MA200 over last 20 bars; negative slope = structural damage
Bonus flags:

trendline_break (bool) — direct answer: is the trend broken?
ma200_declining (bool) — is the 200-day MA slope negative?
Weekly close confirmation adds conviction

from finsell.purchase_timing import score_purchase_timing

df = fetch_ohlcv("MSFT", period="6mo")
pt = score_purchase_timing(df, cost_basis=440.0)
# pt.score=0.61, pt.best_signal="three_weeks_tight + auto_addon"
# pt.sub_scores shows all individual signals

Inputs required:

df — OHLCV data (6mo+ daily recommended)
cost_basis — your weighted average cost per share
Five sub-signals combined:

Signal	What it detects
ma_support	Pullback to 50-day MA, bounce on volume
three_weeks_tight	3 consecutive weekly closes within ~1.5%
flat_base	5+ week sideways, <15% correction
ascending_base	3 pullbacks (10-20%) with rising lows
auto_addon	Stock 2-2.5% above your cost basis

Logic: Uses the best sub-signal (any one pattern is enough). When auto-addon AND a pattern both confirm, they amplify each other.

Score	Meaning
-1.0	No patterns, extended, bad time to add
-0.5	Neutral, no clear signal
0.0	Conditions forming, watch
+0.5	Good add opportunity
+1.0	Strong add signal (pattern + momentum + volume)
Also available standalone:

score_three_weeks_tight(df) — Three-Weeks-Tight pattern
score_ascending_base(df) — Ascending base pattern
